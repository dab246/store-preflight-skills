# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone who participates in this project — maintainers, contributors and users — deserves a
welcoming, harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people using this project.
- Respect confidentiality: never post someone's app name, account details or rejection emails without
  permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sharing techniques for deceiving app review (hidden features, review-environment detection, fake
  metadata). This project exists to help apps comply, not to evade review.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (@dab246). Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so. All reports are handled confidentially.
