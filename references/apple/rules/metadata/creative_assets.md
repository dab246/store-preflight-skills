# Non-compliant creative assets

Guideline 2.3.14 (added 2026-10-16) covers the new image and video **creative assets** shown in
product page headers and search results.

## What to check

- Assets relate to the app's content, functionality or overall brand.
- They follow the standard metadata rules and are suitable for all audiences (4+), whatever the
  app's age rating.
- No specific or discounted **pricing**, no misleading references to other apps, no unverifiable
  claims ("best", "#1", "most downloaded").
- Seasonal or event artwork is removed when the event ends.

## How to detect

Open App Store Connect ▸ your app ▸ product page ▸ creative assets and the new preview tool; review
every locale and both light and dark appearances.

## Resolution

Replace offending assets, keep a short brand style guide for marketing teams, and schedule removal
of time-limited assets.

## Example rejection

> Guideline 2.3.14 - Performance - Accurate Metadata. Your creative assets include pricing
> information, which is not appropriate for creative assets.
