# Unregistered apps or unverified developer identity

## What to check

- **Android developer verification** took effect on **2026-09-30** (Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and
  Thailand first). Play Console now requires apps to be registered to a verified developer; Google
  auto-registered most apps — check Play Console ▸ Home for any remaining apps to avoid **global
  removal** from Google Play.
- Developer account details (legal name, address, D-U-N-S for organizations, contact email/phone)
  are verified and current.
- Account ownership transfers use the official **Transfer ownership** workflow (Account Transfer
  policy, April 2026) — selling or sharing accounts is prohibited.

## Resolution

Review Play Console Home and Developer account ▸ Verification, register remaining apps, and keep
contact information up to date.

## Example rejection

> Your app is not registered to a verified developer and will be removed from Google Play.
