# 🤖 Checklist — Apps for children (Families policy)

- [ ] Target audience & content declared accurately → `../rules/content/families_policy.md`
- [ ] Only Families self-certified ad SDKs; no personalized ads to children
- [ ] No advertising ID, precise location or device identifiers collected from children
- [ ] No anonymous chat features targeting children (since 2026-08-26) → `../rules/content/anonymous_chat.md`
- [ ] IAP and ads appropriate; no deceptive prompts
- [ ] Privacy policy covers children's data; COPPA/GDPR compliance
