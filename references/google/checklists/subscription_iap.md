# 🤖 Checklist — Subscriptions & in-app products

- [ ] Digital goods use Google Play Billing (or an enrolled alternative/external-offers program) → `../rules/monetization/play_billing.md`
- [ ] Play Billing Library 8+ (also inside cross-platform plugins) → `../rules/technical/play_billing_library_version.md`
- [ ] Offer shows price, billing period, renewal, what's included → `../rules/monetization/subscriptions.md`
- [ ] Trials/intro offers show duration and post-trial price
- [ ] Billed price more prominent than per-week/month equivalents
- [ ] Paywall dismissible; no misleading design
- [ ] Link to Play subscription center for cancellation
- [ ] Loot box odds disclosed
- [ ] Purchases acknowledged within 3 days (otherwise auto-refunded)
